At the centre of this area sits **CyclOps**, the product we build ourselves: it
reduces alarm noise to a meaningful event and connects that event to an approved
action. Monitoring products produce the signal; CyclOps focuses on what that
signal means and what happens next.

In this area we start from the organisation's operation rather than a
product. Scope and priority differ in every engagement; we map them together.
