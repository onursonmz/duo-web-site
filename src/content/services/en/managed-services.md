The easiest thing to measure in a managed service is response time; the thing
that matters most is whether the operation gets simpler over time. If the same
alert is cleared by the same manual step every week, the service is working but
not improving. Moving routine work into automation is therefore part of the
defined scope, not a favour outside it.
