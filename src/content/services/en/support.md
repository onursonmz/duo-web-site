  Keeping a delivered solution running: incidents, configuration questions and

  - The rollout is done, the system is in production, and it must be clear who responds when it breaks.

offer:
  - Incident logging, prioritisation and resolution tracking through a defined channel.
  - Answers to configuration questions and good-practice guidance.
  - Upgrade planning with pre-checks and a rollback scenario.
  - Root cause analysis and a permanent fix proposal for recurring incidents.
howWeWork:
  - Every request is recorded; work does not proceed on a verbal agreement.
  - Priority follows affected scope and business impact, not the order requests arrive in.
  - Every change is written down with its rationale and kept reversible.
  - Recurring records are reviewed periodically and tied to a permanent fix.
outcomes:
  - Traceable incident records and resolution history.
  - Configuration and change notes kept current.
  - An executed upgrade plan with its rollback scenario.
  - Root cause analysis output for recurring problems.

The quality of a support engagement is measured less by how fast the first
reply arrives than by how often the same fault comes back. Record keeping is
therefore not paperwork but the work itself: if nobody wrote down which change
broke what, the root cause is rediscovered from scratch every time.
